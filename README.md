Mini City Police — Privacy Policy
Last Updated: October 8, 2026
Developer: AAysit
Contact: privacypolicyforminipolicegame@gmail.com
1. Overview
Mini City Police ("the App") is a child-friendly mobile game developed by AAysit.
We respect the privacy of children, parents and all players. The App is designed so that players can play without creating an account or providing personal information.
2. Information We Collect
Mini City Police does not collect personal information from players through the App.
The App does not require or collect:
- name
- email address
- telephone number
- postal address
- precise location
- contacts
- photographs or videos
- microphone recordings
- camera data
- health information
- advertising identifiers
- user accounts
- chat or messaging information
The App does not contain advertising, behavioral advertising, personalized advertising or analytics services.
The App does not send gameplay data or personal information to servers operated by AAysit.
3. Local Game Data
The App stores gameplay progress locally on the player's device.
This may include:
- stars and progression
- unlocked content
- career progress
- completed missions and activities
- vehicle customization
- player customization
- game settings
- collection progress
- gameplay-related timestamps
This information is used only to provide the game's functionality.
The App does not upload this gameplay data to AAysit or to a remote game server.
Game data is not backed up to the cloud by the App. Removing the App or clearing its application data may remove locally stored game progress.
4. Children's Privacy
Mini City Police is designed for children and families.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
The App does not require a child to provide personal information to play the game.
The App does not provide:
- user-to-user communication
- chat
- voice chat
- social networking
- location sharing
- targeted advertising
- behavioral advertising
We do not sell children's personal information.
5. Parent Area and External Links
The App contains a Parent Area protected by a parent gate.
The Parent Area may contain links to information such as this Privacy Policy.
These links are available only through the Parent Area.
When a parent opens an external link, the device's web browser may open the requested website. The App does not send player gameplay data to that website.
Parents should review the privacy practices of external websites they choose to visit.
6. In-App Purchases
At the time this Privacy Policy was published, Mini City Police's current release does not have an active in-app purchase system.
If in-app purchases are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the applicable Google Play disclosures will be updated before or when those features become available.
Any future purchase would be processed through the applicable Google Play purchase infrastructure. AAysit does not receive or store a player's full payment card information through the App.
7. Third-Party Services
The current release does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, attribution, social-login or other third-party tracking SDKs.
The App uses the Godot game engine and standard Android libraries required to operate the application.
8. Data Sharing
AAysit does not sell, rent or share player personal information with advertisers or data brokers.
The current App does not operate a remote player account or personal-information database.
9. Data Retention and Deletion
Because gameplay data is stored locally on the player's device, players or parents can remove it by:
- uninstalling the App, or
- clearing the App's application data through Android settings.
AAysit does not maintain a remote database containing player gameplay saves.
If a parent contacts us by email, we will receive the email address and information included in that communication. Such information will be used only to respond to the parent's request and will not be used for advertising or profiling.
10. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features or data practices change.
For example, if online services, analytics, advertising or in-app purchases are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will indicate when the policy was most recently changed.
11. Contact
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Mini City Police, please contact:
AAysit
Email: privacypolicyforminipolicegame@gmail.com

- ad
- e-posta adresi
- telefon numarası

- mikrofon kayıtları

- sağlık bilgileri
- reklam kimlikleri

- sohbet veya mesajlaşma bilgileri
